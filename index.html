Lane Guard - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-23
App: Lane Guard (com.naturaldopaminestudio.laneguard)
Publisher: NaturalDopamineStudio ("we", "us")

1. Summary

Lane Guard is a free game supported by ads. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information ourselves. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The advertising companies whose software is built into the game (listed below) collect some device data in order to show ads.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (stages reached, gold, Ether, upgrades, settings, story progress and your ad choice) in local storage on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource, a Unity company), with Unity Ads as the ad network. There are two kinds of ads: rewarded ads you choose to watch in exchange for a bonus, and short ad breaks that can appear between waves later in a run.

To load, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these providers may collect and use the following data from your device:

DataWhy
Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID) and similar device identifiersShow and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad, prevent fraud, personalize ads if you allow it
IP address and approximate location derived from it (such as country or region)Show ads that fit your region, fraud prevention, legal compliance
Ad interactions (ads shown, completed, clicked)Pay for and measure ads, frequency limits
Device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, network type, app version) and diagnostics such as crash and performance dataDeliver ads correctly, keep the ad service working, fix problems

This data is sent to the ad providers over encrypted connections and may be shared with their advertising partners. We do not receive your advertising ID or other personal data from them. The providers act under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource): https://developers.is.com/ironsource-mobile/general/ironsource-mobile-privacy-policy/

4. Your ad choice

When you first open the game, it asks whether ads may be personalized. If you choose not to allow it, you still see ads, but they are not personalized, and the game tells the ad providers that you declined. You can change this at any time in the game under Settings > Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Analytics

The game does not send analytics to us or to a third-party analytics service. Gameplay events are only written to a local debug log on your device. If this ever changes, we will update this policy first.

6. Permissions

The game uses internet access and network state (to load ads), the advertising ID permission (for ads), and, if you enable vibration, the vibration permission. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

7. Children

Lane Guard is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will help.

8. Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete beyond what is on your device. Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland or California), you may have rights over the data held by the ad providers, such as access, deletion, or opting out of personalized advertising or the sale or sharing of data. You can use the choices in section 4, or contact the providers through their privacy policies. You can also contact us and we will point you to the right place.

9. Data retention and security

Local game data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the game. Ad providers keep data according to their own policies. Data in transit to the ad providers is encrypted.

10. International transfers

Ad providers may process data in countries other than your own. Their policies describe the safeguards they use.

11. Changes

If the game or this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
